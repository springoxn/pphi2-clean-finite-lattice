## Executable Checks

Run `python build.py` from the article directory. The script compiles twice with a fixed epoch, checks byte equality and invokes the audit. The audit fails nonzero on source/evidence corruption, unexpected queried axioms, missing expected theorem anchors, citation/reference failures, missing PDF sections or captions, text outside the page, or overfull horizontal boxes. Deliberately corrupted hash, unexpected-axiom and missing-source inputs are rejected as negative controls.

The mathematical arithmetic checks independently recompute the midpoint orders and positive gaps with exact fractions and test the displayed two-dimensional weight inequalities at integer radii. Those arithmetic checks are regression checks for the exposition, not a proof of the general weak-limit theorem.

Final visual inspection renders every PDF page into contact sheets; equations, all tables, references and the conjugation bar are checked as typeset, rather than relying on text extraction alone. A normal Tectonic Fontconfig diagnostic and underfull-box warnings may remain; neither is treated as a mathematical failure or silently converted into evidence of correctness.
