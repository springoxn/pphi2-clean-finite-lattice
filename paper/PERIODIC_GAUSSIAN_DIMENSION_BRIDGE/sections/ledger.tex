The distinction between CONFIRMED and QUALIFIED is deliberate. A finite
symbolic test can check a formula on rational instances but cannot prove its
all-$N$ version. A Lean theorem can certify a fully elaborated declaration
and its dependency footprint, but its scope is still the type that Lean
checks. The article never promotes a source census or a successful PDF build
into mathematical evidence.

The public release includes the exact source hashes, dependency lock, theorem
transcript and build logs used for the Lean statements. Rebuilding the article
is a separate artifact check. The ledger itself is therefore a guide to what
each check establishes, not a replacement for the check.
