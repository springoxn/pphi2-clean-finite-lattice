\title[Periodic Gaussian limits in two and four dimensions]{Periodic Gaussian lattice limits in two and four dimensions:\\a common proof mechanism and its Lean realization}
\author{Yj Zheng}
\date{November 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{60G15, 60B10, 81T08, 68V15}
\keywords{Gaussian free field, lattice approximation, negative Sobolev space, weak convergence, Lean 4, formal verification}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We connect two Lean developments of fixed-volume periodic Gaussian lattice
limits: a two-dimensional limit on a Fourier realization of $H^{-3}$ and a
four-dimensional raw atomic-lift limit on a Fourier realization of $H^{-4}$.
Their common mechanism is a spacing-uniform bound on Fourier-mode moments,
a compact inclusion between negative Sobolev carriers, convergence of finite
Gaussian marginals, and uniqueness from countably many Fourier coordinates.
We give a dimension-independent paper proof for raw lifts in $H^{-s}$ whenever
$s>d/2$, and distinguish it from the two formally verified instantiations.
The comparison makes explicit the site-covariance normalization, lattice
aliases, the zero and Nyquist modes, real support, and the difference between
stored weighted coordinates and physical coefficients. It also explains why
the raw atomic approximation requires a rougher space than the limiting free
field itself. The accompanying sources include typed theorem contracts,
source and dependency hashes, axiom transcripts, symbolic scale checks, and
an audit of the compiled article.
\end{abstract}
