\begin{abstract}
We study the fixed-volume continuum limit of the actual atomic lift of a massive periodic lattice Gaussian field. A Fourier argument proves, for every integer dimension $d\geq1$ and every $s>d/2$, weak convergence in a weighted periodic Fourier realization of $H^{-s}$. We then separate this dimension-uniform analytic theorem from the dimension-specific Lean endpoints available in the public release: dimensions two through five, and the exceptional fixed dimension $d=122409$. The latter is an unconditional whole-sequence theorem for the actual finite-source pushforwards in $H^{-61206}$, with compactness supplied by a stronger $H^{-61205}$ energy estimate. Dimension-parameterized Lean lemmas establish weight summability and comparison facts, but the full convergence theorem is not parameterized by $d$. We record the source normalization, carrier conventions, target identification, proof dependencies, reproducibility commands, and explicit exclusions. In particular, no arbitrary-dimensional Lean convergence theorem, interacting limit, infinite-volume limit, or separate infinite-dimensional Gaussian predicate is claimed.
\end{abstract}
